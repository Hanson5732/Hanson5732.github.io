HypoSelectSimRAG: Enhancing Answer Accuracy in RAG via Multi-Path Self-Consistent Query Translation

IEEE ICCEIC 2025

Zhi Chen, Hanqi Huang (黄瀚祺), Junjie Wu, et al.

The 6th International Conference on Computer Engineering and Intelligent Control (ICCEIC), IEEE, 2025.

Abstract: Existing Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) frameworks struggle when user queries do not directly match the semantic space of the external knowledge corpus, leading to irrelevant context retrieval and factual hallucination. In this paper, we propose HypoSelectSimRAG, a framework coupling multi-path hypothetical document generation with self-consistency query alignment. By varying prompt styles and decoding temperatures (0.1 and 1.1), four distinct hypothetical contexts are generated. A vector-similarity voting mechanism then filters the candidate with the highest query alignment prior to dense retrieval. Extensive experiments on the rag-dataset-1200 benchmark evaluated with RAGAS show that HypoSelectSimRAG improves Factual Correctness to 0.6910, significantly outperforming standard RAG (0.5257) and HyDE (0.6178).
摘要： 针对用户输入非规范或与文档库存在分布偏差时传统 RAG 检索噪声大、模型易产生幻觉的问题，提出 HypoSelectSimRAG 框架。结合 Few-Shot 与问题分类提示工程，在双温度梯度（0.1 / 1.1）下生成四条具有精细度与多样性的虚拟文档路径，并通过余弦相似度自一致性筛选最优假设文档送入向量检索。在 rag-dataset-1200 测试集上经 RAGAS 框架严格评测，事实准确率达 0.6910，较普通 RAG 显著提升 31.4%。

Predicting Electronic and Thermodynamic Properties of Functional Materials with High-Throughput DFT and Graph Neural Networks

Undergraduate Thesis / Monograph

Hanqi Huang (黄瀚祺), Macao Polytechnic University

Research Monograph & Final Year Thesis, 2026.

Abstract: Discovering functional energy materials requires accurately screening electronic and thermodynamic characteristics across vast crystal configuration spaces. We establish an end-to-end automated high-throughput first-principles Density Functional Theory (DFT) computation pipeline based on VASP and Pymatgen on an ArchLinux supercomputer. With 2,598 calculated structures of Perovskite and Heusler crystals, we systematically benchmark Crystal Graph Convolutional Neural Networks (CGCNN) against gradient-boosted trees. CGCNN achieves outstanding accuracy for electronic topological parameters ($R^2 = 0.9635$ for Fermi level, $R^2 = 0.9584$ for band gap), whereas XGBoost excels at thermodynamic formation energy ($R^2 = 0.986$).
摘要： 针对新型能源功能材料筛选成本高昂的瓶颈，在基于 ArchLinux 的超算集群上自主构建“自动化高通量 DFT 第一性原理计算 + 机器学习”混合框架。处理钙钛矿与霍斯勒合金等 2,598 种晶体结构数据，系统对比图神经网络（CGCNN）与表格模型（XGBoost / 随机森林）。CGCNN 在全局晶格拓扑主导的电子性质上表现最优（费米能级 $R^2=0.9635$，带隙 $R^2=0.9584$）；XGBoost 在局域化学键主导的热力学形成能表现最佳（$R^2=0.986$），据此提出体系结构分化筛选方案。
