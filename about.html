About Me

I am an incoming graduate student in Computer Science at The University of Hong Kong (HKU), having earned my Bachelor of Science degree in Computer Science from Macao Polytechnic University (ranked in the Top 5 with a 3.67/4.0 GPA, recipient of the 2024–2025 Macao Fok Ying Tung Foundation Scholarship).

My academic interests lie at the intersection of Machine Learning, Information Retrieval, and High-Throughput Materials Informatics. In our recent research published in IEEE ICCEIC 2025, we designed HypoSelectSimRAG, mitigating retrieval hallucinations by coupling multi-path hypothetical document generation with self-consistent query alignment. In my undergraduate thesis, I developed an automated high-throughput calculation framework combining DFT and Crystal Graph Convolutional Neural Networks (CGCNN) on an ArchLinux supercomputer to predict electronic and thermodynamic material properties with high fidelity.

“I approach engineering with discipline and order: analyzing root causes before acting, letting empirical benchmarks and rigorous data speak for themselves.”

Beyond academia and engineering, sports and music form the core rhythm of my daily life. Regular physical training keeps me composed under pressure, sharpening my patience, focus, and resilience. Whether tackling hard research questions or navigating fast-moving LLM ecosystems, I enjoy learning proactively, communicating with clarity, and delivering meaningful solutions with team partners.

Publications

HypoSelectSimRAG: Enhancing Answer Accuracy in RAG via Multi-Path Self-Consistent Query Translation

IEEE ICCEIC 2025

Z. Chen, Hanqi Huang, J. Wu, et al.

The 6th International Conference on Computer Engineering and Intelligent Control (ICCEIC 2025), IEEE

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) suffers from hallucination when queries deviate from domain corpus distributions. We proposed a multi-path hypothetical translation mechanism combined with temperature scaling (0.1 / 1.1) and vector similarity voting. On the 1,200-sample benchmark evaluated by RAGAS, Factual Correctness achieved 0.6910, substantially outperforming standard RAG (0.5257) and HyDE (0.6178).
