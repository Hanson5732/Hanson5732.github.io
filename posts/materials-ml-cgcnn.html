High-Throughput DFT + CGCNN: Predicting Electronic & Thermodynamic Properties of Functional Crystals

By Hanqi Huang, Macao Polytechnic University

1. Background & Motivation

First-principles Density Functional Theory (DFT) calculations provide quantum-mechanical accuracy for predicting solid-state physical properties. However, high-fidelity DFT calculations (structural relaxation, self-consistent field iterations, band structures, and projected density of states) require immense supercomputing time.

To accelerate crystal screening for renewable energy and semiconductor materials, we built an automated high-throughput calculation and machine learning framework, comparing Crystal Graph Convolutional Neural Networks (CGCNN) with tabular models such as XGBoost and Random Forest.

2. Automated High-Throughput Calculation Pipeline

Running on an ArchLinux-based supercomputing environment, we orchestrated a 4-stage pipeline using VASP and Pymatgen:

  1. Structural Relaxation: High-precision lattice ionic relaxation with strict convergence thresholds ($10^{-5} \text{ eV}$).
  2. Static SCF: Precise charge density calculations using dense Monkhorst-Pack $k$-point grids.
  3. Band Structure Calculations: High-symmetry $k$-path sampling across the Brillouin zone.
  4. Density of States (DOS): Energy discretization around the Fermi window to determine conduction and valence edges.

We curated 2,598 structural candidates of Perovskite and Heusler alloys with fully resolved electronic and thermodynamic ground truth.

3. Machine Learning Architectures & Key Findings

We converted crystal unit cells into multigraphs where nodes represent atomic chemical properties and edges encode interatomic spatial distance vectors.

Key Insight: Architectural Specialization

Orbital Field Matrix (OFM) feature attribution revealed a fundamental principle:

Electronic properties (Fermi level and band gaps) are deeply governed by global lattice topology and quantum spatial symmetry, making graph representations (CGCNN) vastly superior. In contrast, thermodynamic stability (formation energy) is largely driven by localized nearest-neighbor chemical bond enthalpies, where tabular gradient boosted trees with physics-based descriptors deliver extraordinary speed and accuracy.

4. Conclusion & Resources

This research demonstrated that hybridizing domain-specific graph representations with tabular ML allows orders-of-magnitude acceleration in functional material discovery without sacrificing chemical validity.
