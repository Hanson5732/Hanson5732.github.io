High-Throughput DFT & ML for Materials Properties 基于高通量计算与机器学习研究物质电子性质

2025.08 - 2026.04

Constructed an automated high-throughput first-principles (DFT) pipeline with VASP and Pymatgen on an ArchLinux supercomputing cluster. Extracted 2,598 structural candidates of Perovskite and Heusler alloys. Compared Crystal Graph Convolutional Neural Networks (CGCNN) with tabular tree models: CGCNN achieved an $R^2 = 0.9635$ on Fermi Level and $R^2 = 0.9584$ on Band Gap.

在 ArchLinux 超算集群上基于 VASP + Pymatgen 构建四阶段自动化 DFT 高通量计算流水线，处理 2,598 种钙钛矿与霍斯勒晶体结构。对比图模型与表格树模型：CGCNN 在费米能级达 $R^2=0.9635$、带隙达 $R^2=0.9584$，揭示了全局晶格拓扑主导电子性质的物理规律。

HypoSelectSimRAG: Multi-Path Self-Consistent Query Translation HypoSelectSimRAG: 基于多路径自一致性查询转换的 RAG 模型

2025.03 - 2025.08 · IEEE ICCEIC 2025

Designed a novel RAG pipeline to alleviate retrieval hallucinations. Generates diverse hypothetical documents across 4 distinct temperature-guided paths (0.1 / 1.1) and selects the optimal candidate through query-cosine similarity voting. Evaluated on rag-dataset-1200, achieving a Factual Correctness of 0.6910 (vs 0.5257 standard RAG).

设计多路径虚拟文档生成与温度控制（0.1 / 1.1）策略，平衡生成准确性与多样性；对比自一致性投票、评分、向量均值等5种筛选算法，选出与原始查询余弦相似度最高的最优文档投入稠密检索。在 RAGAS 基准评测中，事实准确率大幅提升至 0.6910（标准 RAG 为 0.5257）。
