Materials Informatics · Final Year Project

High-Throughput DFT & ML for Materials Properties

2025.08 - 2026.04 · Macao Polytechnic University

Constructed an automated high-throughput first-principles (DFT) pipeline with VASP and Pymatgen on an ArchLinux supercomputing cluster. Extracted 2,598 structural candidates of Perovskite and Heusler alloys. Compared Crystal Graph Convolutional Neural Networks (CGCNN) with tabular tree models: CGCNN achieved an $R^2 = 0.9635$ on Fermi Level and $R^2 = 0.9584$ on Band Gap, revealing global lattice topology principles.

AI & LLMs · Academic Publication

HypoSelectSimRAG: Multi-Path Self-Consistent Query Translation

2025.03 - 2025.08 · Published at IEEE ICCEIC 2025

Designed a novel RAG pipeline to alleviate retrieval hallucinations. Generates diverse hypothetical documents across 4 distinct temperature-guided paths (0.1 / 1.1) and selects the optimal document through query-cosine similarity voting. Evaluated with LangSmith and Ragas on rag-dataset-1200, achieving a Factual Correctness of 0.6910 (vs 0.5257 standard RAG).
